At the end of August 2026 I spent about twelve days in Portugal: a little over a week in Lisbon and then a few days in Porto. Like the [Japan post](/posts/japan-trip-2026/), this is a day-by-day record, partly for me to come back and reminisce, and partly for anyone looking for recommendations (especially vegetarian food ones) for their own trip :)

<!--more-->

# Lisbon

I was in Lisbon for work, so most of these days aren't typical vacation days. Think of this section as "Lisbon after work hours" plus a weekend.

# Day 1: Landed in Lisbon

Went to see The Odyssey in IMAX at Cinema NOS Colombo. Colombo is a pretty nice and big mall. Before the movie I had an açaí bowl at Oakberry, just outside the mall (would recommend). Also tried the vegan burger at McDonald's (decent). After the movie, a burrito at Taco Bell in the mall. Yes, I ate a lot that day.

![The Odyssey in IMAX, with Portuguese subtitles of course](odyssey.jpeg)

# Day 2

Worked from Heden Workspaces - Rossio (nice coworking space). Lunch at Terraço Editorial, good vegetarian burger. Randomly ended up walking by a Zara store which turned out to be the second-largest in the world.

In the evening went to Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara. Very pretty, not super crowded, and there was a busker performing.

![The view from Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara](miradouro-alcantara.jpeg)

Dinner at NORI Restaurante Japonês, great veg ramen. Gelato at Nannarella after (yum).

# Day 3

Work during the day. Lunch at Honest Greens, and I loved it! Highlights were the sweet potato fries and the Latin Lover plate with halloumi.

![The Latin Lover plate at Honest Greens](honest-greens-latin-lover-bowl.jpeg)

Played padel at Clube de Padel in the evening. Lovely area to walk around with river views.

![The view of the padel courts](padel-court.jpeg)

Walked around that area after padel and then went to LX Factory for dinner. Pizza at Sophia Pizzoteca & Bar (pretty good).

Walked back from LX Factory to my hotel along the waterside, stopping at Praça do Comércio where a busker had almost a mini concert going with people singing along. Such a fun vibe.

# Day 4

Worked out at the Calisthenics Park at Campo dos Mártires da Pátria in the morning. Then met up at MAS Specialty Coffee & Brunch, a colleague's favorite coffee place.

Work, and then ginjinha at "A Ginjinha" in the evening. Went to Praça do Comércio again and then bar hopped. The cocktail highlight was Quattro Teste (their Basque burnt cheesecake was amazing!). Dinner at Damas (kinda mid).

Walked back to the hotel through Alfama, seeing Miradouro da Graça at night on the way. Gelato at the Amorino on Rua Augusta to end the day.

![Lisbon at night from Miradouro da Graça](miradouro-graca.jpeg)

# Day 5

Work, then went for a boat ride.

![Sunset from the boat ride](lisbon-boat-ride.jpeg)

Dinner at Dragon Inn. Decent in terms of the vegetarian food, but the cheesecake wasn't that good (yes, I let my sweet tooth go wild during this trip).

# Day 6

Calisthenics Park at Campo dos Mártires da Pátria again in the morning. After work, went to Absent Lisbon to play board games. Interesting concept: you can't use your phone while you're in the café. Good protein cheesecake too (does it even count as a dessert if it's helping you fulfill your protein goals?).

Went to Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara again, then walked by Largo do Calhariz on the way to Miradouro de Santa Catarina. Had a burger at Dallas Burger (a chain), great place for a veg burger.

Walked back to the hotel past Pink Street and the Time Out Market (fun vibe), then through the streets of Bairro Alto and finally to Praça do Comércio to see if the same busker was there again (he wasn't 😢).

![Inside the Time Out Market](time-out-market.jpeg)

# Day 7: Cascais

Went to Parque Eduardo VII and had lunch at the Honest Greens near it. Shifted hotels and then went to Cascais. Chilled by the beach, swam, and walked around the town.

![Cascais](cascais.jpeg)

# Day 8: Alfama, Belém, and The Weeknd

Went to Alfama again and saw Miradouro de Santa Luzia and Miradouro das Portas do Sol. Walked around Alfama up to Castelo de São Jorge (didn't go inside though, the queue was long).

![Me at Miradouro das Portas do Sol](miradouro-portas-do-sol.jpeg)

Headed to Belém and had a pastel de nata at Pastéis de Belém. By far the best one I had, and it lived up to the hype.

In the evening, attended The Weeknd's concert at Estádio do Restelo.

![The Weeknd's concert at Estádio do Restelo](weeknd-concert.jpeg)

# Porto

# Day 9: Lisbon to Porto

Took the train from Lisbon to Porto. Reached São Bento station and looked at the azulejos there. Dropped my bags at the hotel and went to Espécie for lunch. It was okay-ish, not as good as I had expected based on the reviews.

Strolled around and went to "[the most beautiful McDonald's in the world](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VHHmNnoHMXSndBJ89)".

![The most beautiful McDonald's in the world](mcdonalds.jpeg)

Walked across the bridge to the Gaia side and sat at Jardim do Morro to watch the sunset with amazing live music by [Vitor Mayer](https://www.instagram.com/vitor_mayer/?hl=en).

![Sunset from Jardim do Morro](first-evening-at-jardim.jpeg)

Dinner at [Cult of Pita](https://maps.app.goo.gl/1TserHmwLGYuxau47). Amazing! (Remember this name, it'll come up again. And again.)

![Dinner at Cult of Pita](cult-of-pita.jpeg)

# Day 10: Douro Valley

Had booked the Douro Valley tour from [Living Tours](https://www.livingtours.com/en/). Nice experience, but the tour could've been better. It was supposed to be an English-only tour, yet the guide kept explaining everything in both Portuguese and English. Still, some really nice views throughout!

![Views during the Douro Valley tour](douro-valley.jpeg)

Most of the day went into the tour. Came back and ate at McDonald's, and since it was raining I went back to the hotel. Then Cult of Pita again for dinner.

# Day 11

Living Tours gives a free walking tour coupon to people who do any tour with them, so I went on the free walking tour in the morning. Fun experience, but again the guide seemed more interested in taking us to shops where we were incentivized to buy things.

The tour ended by the waterside in Ribeira, so I strolled around there for a bit.

![Ribeira from the river](ribeira.jpeg)

Did the 6 bridges boat tour and walked around the neighborhood after that.

Took the train to [Guará Restaurant & Bakery](https://maps.app.goo.gl/iUtG7z2t7ScPKedx8) in Gaia to have a vegetarian francesinha. It was interesting to try but I wasn't a fan, too much tomato in the taste for my liking.

![The vegetarian francesinha at Guará](francesinha.jpeg)

Went to Livraria Lello. They were kind enough to move my appointment to an earlier slot. Spent some time there, and don't miss the Dua Lipa section in the new block!

![Inside Livraria Lello](livraria-lello.jpeg)

![The Dua Lipa section in Livraria Lello's new block](dua-lipa-section.jpeg)

Dropped by the hotel to keep the book I had bought, then got gelato from [Gelataria La Copa](https://maps.app.goo.gl/uDmJU1PVr1jrZq8L6) and walked to Jardim do Morro again to watch the sunset and listen to Vitor Mayer.

![Back at Jardim do Morro for another sunset with Vitor Mayer](second-evening-at-jardim.jpeg)

Dinner at Cult of Pita (yes, I was obsessed).

# Day 12

My train back to Lisbon was in the afternoon. Went to [Leitaria da Quinta do Paço](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zhiUK1vHx9sf3g2D6) for an eclair (it was quite good!) and sat there to do some work. Strolled around the [Urban market](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ggg65d7KmTkrkYv68), which I discovered on the free walking tour the day before.

Took the train back to Lisbon and went to Honest Greens once again for dinner. Crashed at a friend's place for a few hours before my flight back to India.
